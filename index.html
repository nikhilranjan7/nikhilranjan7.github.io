    Work 03

    Four releases from the LLM360 programme at MBZUAI IFM. All four are fully open: data, weights, checkpoints and code.

    TxT360

    Oct 2024 · 15T+ tokens · #1 trending dataset on Hugging Face for several weeks

    99 Common Crawl snapshots and 14 curated sources (Wikipedia, arXiv, PubMed, StackExchange, legal and government text, among others) deduplicated globally, so every document appears once across the whole corpus rather than once per source. The duplicate counts are kept as metadata, which is what lets a training run upweight the text that matters instead of guessing. Released with the weighting recipe. The fifth most downloaded open pretraining corpus on Hugging Face, ahead of EleutherAI, the Allen Institute and NVIDIA, the basis of Gaperon's English data at Inria, and part of the 7.1T-token pretraining corpus of AMD's Instella-MoE.

    Technical reportDataset

    MegaMath

    Apr 2025 · 371B tokens · COLM 2025

    Mathematical text is a sliver of the web and standard extractors destroy it. MegaMath re-extracts math documents from Common Crawl with math-aware processing, recovers mathematical code, and adds synthetic data. The most downloaded open math corpus.
